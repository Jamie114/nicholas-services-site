# Calculator review — 2 November 2026

These are indicative models, not lender calculators, approvals, personal tax advice or a replacement for current professional checks. Saved cases may recalculate differently after this update. Review previous scenarios before relying on them.

## Two properties

The target is a combined LVR across the properties. The model treats the loan approval limit as a cap on the new purchase loan, not on existing mortgage debt. Actual combined debt is existing mortgage after any draw plus the actual new loan plus estimated LMI. Equity draw to 80% never reduces the existing mortgage. The same interest rate and remaining term are assumed on both loans, all new borrowing is P&I and financing and security structures are simplified. A new loan cannot exceed the cash needed to complete, while excess equity release remains debt. Optional annual running costs for BOTH properties must be entered by the user, including land tax if relevant. No property costs or land tax are automatically inferred from total market value. An assumed $2,000 is included for transfer fees, and ordinary owner-occupier or investment duty is estimated using the selected state and property use; first-home concessions are not modelled in this tab. LMI is illustrative and not a lender quote. If a waiver is selected but the modelled LVR exceeds the cap, LMI is estimated; the target is not automatically lowered in this two-property model. Tax and negative gearing benefits are not calculated. Income alone is not serviceability assessment.

## Refinancing

Current term means the remaining term. Both loans are assumed P&I at fixed entered rates and fees are assumed paid upfront. LMI, when charged, is estimated and capitalised; its principal and financing interest are included in the cost comparisons. Interest and upfront-cost differences compare equal elapsed time using each loan's own repayment frequency; a positive figure indicates lower interest and costs on the new loan. Whole-term interest comparisons are sensitive to the repayment term. The simple repayment payback compares upfront cost against annualised repayment change and ignores differences in remaining balances; do not treat it as a total-wealth break-even point. Extra borrowing makes like-for-like comparisons unavailable. Future rates, ongoing fees, offsets, early repayments, tax and break costs other than those entered are not modelled.

## Borrowing estimate

The profiles and living-expense tables are invented, illustrative assumptions, not a bank's actual serviceability model or HEM. Gross salary and shaded other/rental income are modelled using simplified 2026–27 Australian resident income tax rates and a flat 2% Medicare levy. Low income offsets, levy thresholds, tax residency, HELP tax treatment, deductions, rental expenses and individual circumstances are not modelled. Shaded rent and other income are allocated to applicant 1 for this approximation. Enter a separate monthly HELP commitment and lender-assessed existing loan repayments where applicable; input all liabilities. Defaults are assumptions, including a 3 percentage point buffer, and do not imply lender policy. Purchase price assumes the required deposit exists and excludes duty and other purchase costs. Validate against actual lender calculators, policy and current year tax rules.
